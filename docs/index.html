How the score works

Each headline's title and summary is scored from −1 (very bad) to +1 (very good). Zero is in the middle. We call it good news at 0.05 or above, and bad news at −0.05 or below. A few strong words can nudge the result, and scores are capped at ±1. We don't read the full article, so sarcasm and context can still fool it.
